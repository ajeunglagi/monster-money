# Demo video script — Monster Money (under 3 minutes)

Record a screen capture of the live app (phone or desktop). Speak or add
captions in simple English. Total target: ~2 minutes 20 seconds.

## Shots
- **0:00–0:15 — Hook.** Show the home screen. "Money is scary. Meet the
  monsters that eat your budget. This is Monster Money — a budget tracker
  where every spending category is a monster with a health bar."
- **0:15–0:45 — The monsters.** Scroll the monster grid. Point at Rent
  Goblin, Food Fiend, Fun Phantom. "Green means healthy. Red means almost
  eaten. SLAIN means over budget."
- **0:45–1:20 — Feed a monster.** Open the form, log "$25 — Food Fiend —
  Midnight pizza". Watch the Food Fiend health bar drop and the totals
  update. "One tap, and every number on the page moves."
- **1:20–1:50 — Projection and charts.** Show the projected month-end
  figure and the warning, then the donut and the 14-day bars. "It tells you
  plainly if you are on pace to overspend — before it happens."
- **1:50–2:15 — Achievements and privacy.** Show an earned achievement.
  "No account, no bank link, no tracking. Your data never leaves your
  browser. Export a backup whenever you like."
- **2:15–2:25 — Close.** "Monster Money. Tame your money monsters. Built
  with Kiro for Kiroween 2026."

## Recording notes
- Record at 1080p if possible; keep the mouse still while talking.
- Before recording, click "Summon demo data" so the charts and monsters
  have something to show, then reset the month after if you want a clean
  start on camera. (Or record the demo-data click itself — it is a nice
  moment.)
- Upload to YouTube as **Public** (unlisted will not count — Devpost needs
  a public link judges can open).
